**[marcaionline.com.br](https://marcaionline.com.br)** · scheduling and ordering platform for small businesses such as barbershops, salons, sports arenas and bakeries.

Each business gets its own subdomain, branding, services and schedule. Customers book or order from their phone with no account and no app install (PWA).

Try it as a customer: [book at Salão Aurora](https://salao-aurora.marcaionline.com.br/) or [order at Forno & Afeto](https://forno-e-afeto.marcaionline.com.br/), two sample businesses inside the live system.
